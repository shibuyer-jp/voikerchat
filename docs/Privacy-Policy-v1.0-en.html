  • To deliver and optimize advertising, including serving region-appropriate ads
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To prevent misuse and maintain security

3. AI-Generated Conversation and Voice Read-Aloud

The conversation feature of the App sends the messages you enter to the servers of our AI service provider (Anthropic, PBC) in order to generate responses.

  • What is sent is the text you enter in the conversation feature.
  • It is sent to the extent necessary for learning purposes and is used to generate responses.
  • Please do not enter unnecessary personally identifying information into conversations.

If you use the voice read-aloud feature, the text being read aloud (such as the AI's response) is sent to the servers of our text-to-speech provider (OpenAI, L.L.C.). Only the text being read is sent, and it is used solely for voice synthesis.

12. How to Delete Your Voikerchat Account and Data

Users of Voikerchat can delete their account and all learning data at any time using the following steps:

  1. Open the App and go to the "Settings" screen
  2. Select "Delete account"
  3. Confirm deletion in the confirmation dialog

Deletion is carried out promptly (normally within a few seconds) and cannot be undone.

This operation permanently deletes the following data:

  • Account information (anonymous authentication ID)
  • Learning data (conversation history, test scores, scenes used, etc.)
  • Usage data (number of sessions per day, number of ads viewed, etc.)
  • Notification history (records of notifications delivered by the App)
  • Purchase and billing data (subscription purchase and renewal status)
  • Advertising identifier
  • Approximate location

Retention period: except for information we are required by law to retain, the data above is deleted immediately upon the deletion request and is not retained.
